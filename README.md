# Fall-2023-MEM-611
Conductive Heat Transfer

## Course Summary

Fall 2023 MEM 611 (Conductive Heat Transfer) covers the fundamentals and engineering applications of heat conduction. Topics include steady and transient conduction, one- and multi-dimensional heat transfer, material thermal properties, and boundary conditions. Students learn analytical methods (separation of variables, similarity solutions), approximate techniques (integral methods), and numerical approaches (finite difference and finite element methods). Coursework emphasizes problem solving and project-based applications such as thermal management, insulation design, transient response analysis, simulations in Python and COMSOL, and field-specific scientific literature analysis.
